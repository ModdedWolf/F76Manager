WmaDecoder is a managed port of parts of FFmpeg's Windows Media Audio
v1/v2 decoder (libavcodec/wma.c, libavcodec/wmadec.c, libavcodec/wmadata.h,
libavcodec/aactab.c scale-factor tables, libavformat/xwma.c container logic).
